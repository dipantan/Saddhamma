
# Saddhamma App Feature Analysis (Combined Reddit Threads)
Last updated: 2026-06-26

## ✅ What Your App Already Does Well
1. **Core Offline Reader**: Fast, clean, distraction-free offline sutta access
2. **Bilingual View**: Side-by-side English/Pāli display
3. **Gradual Training Check-in**: Tracks Sense Restraint, Moderation in Eating, Wakefulness, Mindfulness & Clear Comprehension, and Precepts (binary yes/no)
4. **Meditation Timer**: Presets, breathing animation, notes, halfway bells (you already have the integrated timer users requested!)
5. **Practice Tracking**: Reading history, meditation logs, check-ins
6. **Gamification**: Streak tracking, badges for practice consistency
7. **User Annotations**: Notes and highlights per segment and per sutta
8. **Dark Mode**: Full dark/light theme support
9. **Translator Credits**: Shows "Translation by Bhikkhu Sujato" etc.
10. **Legacy HTML Fallback**: Supports texts without segmented translations
11. **Bookmarks**: Save favorite suttas
12. **Copy Text**: Copy entire sutta or individual segments
13. **Open Source**: Your project is open source! ✅
14. **Path Context**: Your menu structure already reflects canonical collections (DN, MN, SN, AN, KN)

## 🚨 Critical Doctrinal Feedback (From Bhikkhu Anigha)
1. **Avoid Over-Gamification**: Your app has badges and streaks—this is okay for basic consistency, but **avoid wording that implies jhāna/samādhi is a "goal" or metric**. Your timer is framed well ("Mindful Meditation") but ensure no text says "aim for jhāna in 30 minutes".
2. **Keep Tracking Binary**: Your check-in is already binary (yes/no)—great! Don't add sliders or ratings for "how well" you did sense restraint or samādhi (note: some users suggested 1-5 star ratings for traits like Generosity, but we should keep it binary to follow monastic advice).
3. **Precept Clarity**: Your app tracks "Precepts Observed"—consider clarifying that this refers to the 5 lay precepts (kamesu micchacara), not absolute celibacy (abrahmacariya) unless explicitly for monastics.

## ❌ Missing High-Priority Features
From all three Reddit threads combined:
1. **In-Sutta Search ("Find on Page")**: Your app has global search for suttas, but no in-text search within a specific sutta
2. **Inline Pali Dictionary**: Tap/click a Pāli term to see instant definition (core SuttaCentral feature that users say is a dealbreaker)
3. **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Read suttas aloud
4. **Meditation Timer Enhancements**:
   - Continue counting after bell (over-time support)
   - Do Not Disturb automation (toggle DND when timer starts/stops)
   - iOS Live Activity support (lock screen timer)
5. **Donation Link ("Buy Me a Coffee")**: Users requested a way to support development
6. **Data Backup/Import/Export**: For notes, highlights, logs, check-ins
7. **Persistent Bilingual View Setting**: Ensure the user's preferred view (English-only / Pāli-only / side-by-side) is a global persistent setting that doesn't reset between suttas!
8. **Clean Numerical Sorting**: Verify suttas are sorted numerically (MN 1, MN 2, MN 3...) not erratically
9. **Path Map UI**: A canonical "Map of the Path" visualizing the Gradual Training sequence (Dāna → Sīla → ... → Vipassanā) with sutta links
10. **Daily Sutta Notification**: "Sutta of the Day" push notifications

## 🟢 Good to Keep
- Your gradual training check-in is already binary (no ratings) ✅
- Your meditation timer is framed as a space for reflection, not a "state goal" ✅
- You have a solid foundation of core reader features ✅
- You're already open source! ✅

## 🧭 New Inspiration: "Path Map" UI
Consider adding a visual "Path Map" screen that shows the canonical Gradual Training sequence (from DN 2, MN 53, MN 27) with:
- Direct sutta links to relevant texts
- Canonical quotes for each stage
- Simple checkmarks for daily practice consistency (no ratings/scores)
