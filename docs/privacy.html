Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Saddhamma is built as an Open Source app. This service is provided by the Saddhamma Project at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

Saddhamma is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app does not collect any personal information, tracking data, or analytics.

All data, including your bookmarks and reading settings, is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to any external servers.

Data Syncing

The app connects to GitHub's servers solely to download and update the Sutta database. No personal data is shared during this process.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at dipantan755@gmail.com or through our GitHub repository issues page.
